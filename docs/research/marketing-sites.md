# Competitor marketing sites

Checked 2026-09-25. Reference only; decisions live in `docs/design/features/marketing-site.md`.

| Site | Structure | Language | Pricing | Trust / contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [RedPoint](https://www.redpoint.co.tz/home) | Video hero → 3 steps → packages → features → template gallery → value props | English, some Swahili terms | White / Black ("Best Selling") / Red, per card | No testimonials or numbers; two phone numbers; agent network |
| [Kadijanja](https://kadijanja.co.tz/) | Hero ("Send Test Card", "Create Event") → stats → features → 29 sample cards → 4 tiers | English top, Swahili pricing ("Bei Za Kadi Za Mialiko") | Basic → Executive, per card | Placeholder counters ("0K+ invitations"); support phone; OTP sign-up |
| [Sherehe Digital](https://www.sherehe.co.tz/) | Hero slider → recent events gallery → packages | English/Swahili toggle | Silver → Platinum (2,000–4,000), per card, 50/50 payment | Real past events with venues; two phone numbers |

Patterns: one primary call to action; 3-step "how it works"; per-card tiers with a highlighted middle plan; sample card gallery; phone numbers in plain view.
Gaps D-Card can use: Swahili-first copy; fast pages without video heroes; honest trust signals (no fake counters); pricing that shows contributions (michango) in every plan; real card samples from the product.
